# Productive-Families-Program-in-Sharjah-Challenges-and-Prospects
Explores the challenges, characteristics, and prospects of the Productive Families Program (PFP) in Sharjah, UAE, looking at how home-based businesses contribute to economic diversification and social empowerment.
Author: Alina Turekova
Abstract:
In this study, I explore the operational characteristics, lifecycle challenges, and growth prospects of home-based enterprises operating under the Productive Families Program (PFP) in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates. Adopting a qualitative exploratory research design, I conducted semi-structured interviews in English and Arabic with eleven purposefully sampled participants and analyzed the transcripts using a six-step thematic content analysis. Five core themes emerged—Business Structure & Program Characteristics, Business Lifecycle Challenges, Technology Adoption, Skills and Competencies, and Marketing Mix Challenges—revealing that while the program provides a low-cost entry gateway to micro-entrepreneurship, participants experience inconsistent access to institutional support. Furthermore, home-based entrepreneurs face persistent hurdles in startup capital, scaling quality control, uneven digital adoption, and skill gaps in formal marketing and financial management.
